Solving Rush Hour Overcrowding in Ecoland

Pup Coffee Ecoland is a favorite study and rest stop for students, remote workers, and parishioners visiting St. John Paul II Parish. During peak hours (3 PM – 9 PM), customers often wait in long queues and struggle to find available study tables with power outlets.

Our web application gives you real-time visibility over seating capacity and lets you pre-order your favorites ahead of time.
